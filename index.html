Includes customer phone/mobile number, three distinct product recommendations, three-language customer messages (English / Sinhala / Tamil), WhatsApp click-to-chat and normal SMS options when a valid number is available. Data protection: the uploaded transaction file is processed in this browser and is not uploaded or stored by this page. Internal stock status stays sales-team-only and is never included in customer-facing WhatsApp/SMS messages. Use only customer data you are authorized to process.

Recommendation Diversity Rule

Primary + Alternative 1 + Alternative 2 are designed to be different products. The engine avoids repeating the same SKU/product and prioritizes different product categories. When a customer has already purchased most new categories, additional catalogue products are used to preserve product-level diversity.
